## What to build

Manage `~/.hammerspoon` directly as plain files, taken from the keyboard repo as it is today, without its tooling.

- Files: `init.lua`, `windows.lua`, `status-message.lua`. Copied from `~/repos/keyboard/hammerspoon` and adjusted:
  - `init.lua` keeps the `hs` command-line install into `~/.local`, the reload hotkey, one change watcher on the config folder, `require("windows")` (module names lose the `keyboard.` prefix), and the ready alert. Drop the symlink-following second watcher and the EmmyLua spoon loading.
  - `windows.lua` loses the two lines that pause and resume space-fn while the modal is active; nothing else changes.
  - `status-message.lua` unchanged apart from the require path.
- Space-fn, its engine, the spec suite, Makefile, luarocks, lefthook, stylua and EmmyLua stubs are not copied. The archived keyboard repo keeps them.
- `~/.hammerspoon/Spoons` is excluded from management (never `exact_`, and listed in `.chezmoiignore`).
- The files are plain, home-relative, and re-addable. No script is needed; Hammerspoon reloads on file change.
- On a machine without Hammerspoon the files are still written; they are inert.
